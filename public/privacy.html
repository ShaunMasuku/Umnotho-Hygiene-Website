Privacy Notice

How website enquiries are handled.

This notice explains how Umnotho Hygiene (Pty) Ltd uses personal information submitted through this website's enquiry form.

Information we collect

When you contact us, the website may collect your name, organisation, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, location, service of interest and the message you choose to send.

Why we use it

We use this information to receive, assess and respond to your enquiry, prepare or discuss a quotation, and communicate with you about the service you requested.

How the website handles enquiries

The website does not require a customer database for contact-form submissions. The submitted information is transmitted to an email delivery provider and emailed to info@umnothohygiene.co.za. Messages may then remain in Umnotho Hygiene's business email systems as part of normal correspondence and record keeping.

Sharing and service providers

We only use service providers needed to operate the website and deliver the enquiry, such as website hosting and transactional email services. They process information for the purpose of providing those services.

Your choices

You may choose not to use the website form and instead contact Umnotho Hygiene directly by telephone, WhatsApp or email. You may also contact us regarding access to, correction of or questions about personal information you previously supplied.
