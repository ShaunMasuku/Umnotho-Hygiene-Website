About Us

Professional support for cleaner, safer facilities.

Umnotho Hygiene is a South African hygiene and waste management company providing reliable, compliant and client-focused services to healthcare, education, commercial and industrial clients.

Who we are

Practical service. Professional standards. Long-term partnerships.

We support facilities with safe, practical and professional solutions that help protect people, improve cleanliness and strengthen operational standards.

Our service model is designed to support individual sites, multi-site organisations and clients that need coordinated hygiene, waste and facility-support services.
